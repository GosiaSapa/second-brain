# About me

## Context
I am a business analyst working at Heineken. I support a Salesforce-based product used by key users across around 40 countries, with a strong focus on sales and commercial operations. The main users depend on the application to work reliably, consistently, and at scale across regions. The business is under pressure from competitors that are also investing in digital and operational tooling, while the product must balance usability, reliability, and regional complexity.

## How to work with me
- Be direct but respectful. I value honesty and clear trade-offs more than soft wording.
- Keep answers conversational, but grounded. A short context paragraph followed by the recommendation is ideal.
- I am strongest in business analysis: turning complexity into clear decisions, metrics, and priorities.
- I want the AI to challenge assumptions early, especially when we are optimizing for speed, quality, or adoption without enough evidence.
- I respond well to practical, evidence-based recommendations tied to user value and business impact.
- I want help strengthening my analytics and data thinking, especially when evaluating product and operational decisions.
- When framing recommendations, connect them to user pain points, business constraints, and measurable impact rather than only technical elegance.
- I want support that pushes me to act, but not in a way that ignores real constraints or stakeholder realities.

## Current goal
Learn how to use AI agents and build applications, especially turning business problems into practical tools and products.
